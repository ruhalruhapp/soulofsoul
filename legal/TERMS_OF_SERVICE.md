**The Service is NOT a substitute for emergency services.** If you are experiencing a medical or psychiatric emergency, call 911 (US), go to your nearest emergency room, or call the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988).

### 2.3 AI Companion Boundaries
The AI companion is an automated wellness tool, NOT a therapist or licensed professional. It:
